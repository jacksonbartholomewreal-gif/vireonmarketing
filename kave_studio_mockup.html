About the Studio

Not Just a Haircut.

Kave Studio is a private barbershop nestled in the heart of Downtown Pomona, CA. This isn't a walk-in shop — it's an exclusive, by-appointment environment where every detail is intentional.

From the moment you sit in the chair to the moment you walk out, the focus is entirely on you. Detailed cuts, custom fades, and elevated finishes are the standard — not the exception.
